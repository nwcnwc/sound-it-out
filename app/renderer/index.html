Setup

One recording session that matters, one optional extra, and where things are kept.

  1. 1

    Record the sounds

    The 42 letter sounds — sss, mmm, t. Everything is built from these, and an isolated sound is exactly what a computer voice gets wrong — so this ten-minute session is the one that matters. Until it is done, a recorded human starter voice fills in. Read the recording guide first — it explains the one rule that really matters.

  2. 2

    Stories in your voice

    Optional. Anything on the list you have not recorded is read by the built-in voice. With this, it is read in your voice instead: read the passage below once (about six minutes), download the voice pack, and every unrecorded word or line sounds like you.

    One download, once. Everything stays on this computer — nothing is uploaded and nothing leaves the house. It is slow to generate on a laptop, which is one more reason recording yourself is better where you can.

Keep your recordings safe
