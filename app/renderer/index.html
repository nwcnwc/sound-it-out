The later levels

Levels 1 to 5 use your own recordings, exactly as you said them — nothing is made up by the computer. That's a year or more of material, and it needs nothing extra installing.

The later levels use a whole dictionary and full sentences, which is far more than anyone could record by hand. For those, the computer can learn the shape of your voice from your recordings and read the rest in it.

It's a 2–4 GB download, it happens once, and it's all on this computer — nothing is uploaded and nothing leaves the house. You don't need it to start, and you won't need it for a long while.
