# Installing Sound It Out

You do not need a GitHub account, and you do not need to know anything technical.
This is a download and a couple of clicks.

---

## 1. Download it

Go to **[the releases page](https://github.com/nwcnwc/sound-it-out/releases/latest)**
and download the file for your computer:

| Your computer | Download the file ending in |
|---|---|
| Mac (2020 or newer) | `-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac (older, Intel) | `-x64.dmg` |
| Windows | `.exe` |
| Linux | `.AppImage` |

Not sure which Mac you have? Click the  menu → **About This Mac**. If it says
**Apple M1/M2/M3/M4**, use `arm64`. If it says **Intel**, use `x64`.

The file is large — around 500 MB — because the whole thing works offline. Nothing
is downloaded again later, and it never needs the internet to run.

---

## 2. Open it — and expect one warning

The app is not signed with Apple or Microsoft (that costs a few hundred pounds a
year, which is hard to justify for a family project). Your computer will therefore
warn you the first time, and **only** the first time.

### On a Mac

1. Open the `.dmg` you downloaded.
2. Drag **Sound It Out** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Try to open it. macOS will refuse, saying it cannot check it for malicious
   software. That is expected.
4. Go to  → **System Settings** → **Privacy & Security**.
5. Scroll down. You will see a line about Sound It Out being blocked, with an
   **Open Anyway** button. Click it.
6. Confirm. It opens.

From then on it opens normally, like any other app.

### On Windows

Windows SmartScreen will say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info**,
then **Run anyway**. Once only.

---

## 3. First things to set up

**Put in your real names.** Open the **Words** tab. It comes with example names —
Alex, Mum, Dad, Nana, Grandad — and you should replace them with your actual ones:
your child's name, brothers and sisters, pets, anyone they see every day.

This matters more than any other setting. The research on reading and Down
syndrome is specific about it: children learn words they already care about far
faster than words chosen by a curriculum.

**Then make a video.** Go to **Make a video**, pick a level, and press
**Play on this screen**. It takes a few minutes to prepare — you can leave it and
come back.

**When you are ready, record your voice.** See [RECORDING.md](RECORDING.md). It is
about 40 minutes with a phone, and it replaces the computer voice with your own for
everything your child hears at the early levels. It is the single biggest
improvement you can make.

---

## Updating later

The app checks for a new version when it starts and tells you if there is one. It
never updates itself behind your back, and it never interrupts what you are doing.

**Everything you make is kept when you update** — your word list, your recordings,
your settings, and every video you have made. They are stored separately from the
app itself, so replacing the app cannot touch them.

---

## If something goes wrong

Videos are saved to a folder called **Sound It Out** in your home folder. If the app
will not start at all, or a video will not play, the most useful thing you can send
is what the app said on screen, word for word.
